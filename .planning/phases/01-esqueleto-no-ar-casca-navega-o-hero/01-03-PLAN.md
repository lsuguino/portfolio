## Phase Goal

**As a** recrutador que recebe o link em um chat ou e-mail, **I want to** ver um preview com título, descrição e imagem ao invés de um card em branco, **so that** o primeiro contato com o portfólio já pareça profissional antes mesmo de eu abrir.

## Plan Objective

<objective>
Make the shared-link preview work: add `<title>`, meta description, and Open Graph / Twitter-card tags to `index.html`, ship a non-blank placeholder `og:image` (1200x630) and a minimal favicon, and keep the preview text consistent with the hero identity (name, role, pitch) and the real contact email.

Builds on Plan 01's `index.html` and hero content. It is an independent expansion slice from Plan 02 (different files — no overlap with the nav/shell work), so it runs in the same wave. META-01 is the primary requirement; it reinforces HERO-01 by making the role/pitch the first thing a recruiter sees in a link unfurl, even before opening the page.

Purpose: Satisfy META-01 (title + description + Open Graph) and the "preview works when shared" half of ROADMAP criterion 5. Mitigates Pitfall 10 (blank social preview) and Pitfall 13 (missing favicon).
Output: A shareable page whose link preview shows title, description, and image.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| static host → social scraper | OG tags and og:image are fetched by third-party unfurl bots (LinkedIn/WhatsApp); public, read-only. |
| published email → scrapers | The contact email in meta is harvestable by scrapers. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1-20 | Information Disclosure | contact email in meta/markup | low | accept | The email `leonardosuguino@gmail.com` (D-09) is intentionally public for recruiter contact; harvesting risk is acknowledged and accepted for a portfolio. No obfuscation required at this scale. |
| T-1-21 | Spoofing | og:url / og:image pointing off-origin | low | mitigate | og:url is set to the project's own deployment URL and og:image to a same-origin `public/` asset, so the unfurl represents this site and not a spoofed target. |
| T-1-22 | Tampering | index.html meta content | low | mitigate | All meta values are static literals matching `content.ts`; no dynamic/user-supplied content is injected into the head. |
</threat_model>
