## Phase Goal

**As a** recrutador, **I want to** usar uma navegação fixa no topo para pular suavemente às seções de projetos e sobre mim, **so that** eu encontre o que procuro em uma página única sem me perder.

## Plan Objective

<objective>
Expand the Walking Skeleton into the full responsive single-page shell: a fixed top navbar with smooth-scroll anchor links, placeholder `#projetos` and `#sobre` sections (correct anchor IDs so nothing is broken at launch — D-05), and a minimal footer, all verified to behave at 375 / 768 / 1280px with no horizontal scroll and WCAG-AA contrast.

Builds directly on Plan 01's scaffold, theme tokens, and Hero. This is a vertical expansion, not a layer: after it, a visitor can actually navigate the page.

Purpose: Satisfy LAYOUT-01 (responsive single page), LAYOUT-02 (dark minimalist + AA contrast across breakpoints), and LAYOUT-03 (fixed nav + smooth-scroll anchors to placeholder sections).
Output: A navigable, responsive single-page shell.
</objective>
